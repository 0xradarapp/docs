# Pricing

0xRadar offers a tiered model designed to scale from prototypes to production.

## API Tiers

| Feature | Free | Dev | Pro |
|---------|------|-----|-----|
| Rate limit | 10/min | 100/min | 600/min |
| Daily cap | 100 | 10,000 | 100,000 |
| Chains | All 7 | All 7 | All 7 |
| Commercial use | No | Yes | Yes |
| Support | Community | Email | Priority |
| Price | $0 | $29/mo via RapidAPI | $99/mo via RapidAPI |

## Crypto-only Lifetime tier

Pay once with cryptocurrency for unlimited lifetime access to Chrome extension Pro features.

Accepted:
- **BTC** (Bitcoin)
- **ETH** (Ethereum + Arbitrum + Base + Optimism)
- **SOL** (Solana)
- **BNB** (BSC)
- **KAS** (Kaspa)

Why crypto only? Lower transaction fees (0.5% vs 5% for cards) — savings passed to you.

## How to upgrade

1. Log in to [0xradar.app](https://0xradar.app)
2. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
3. Select your plan and complete checkout
